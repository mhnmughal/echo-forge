# LEVEL_DESIGN — Echo Forge

Status: DRAFT. Each stage gets: title · teaching goal · emitters · components ·
receiver rules · timing · starting config · adjustment par · intended solution ·
special mechanics. Filled as levels are authored.

## Series A — Reflection
- **L0 Sound Check** — tutorial. Emitter + 1 reflector + 1 receiver. Teaches select,
  rotate, PULSE, observe. No rating. Persists tutorial-complete flag.
- **L1 First Echo** — 1 emitter, 1 reflector, 1 receiver. Single redirect.
- **L2 Corner Tone** — 2 reflectors, 2 direction changes. Spatial chaining.
- **L3 Silent Wall** — introduce Absorber; kill one unwanted branch/path.
- **L4 Echo Path** — multiple reflectors + 1 movable socket, tight adjustment par.
  First mastery puzzle.

## Series B — Resonance
- **L5 Split Signal** — introduce Splitter; one emitter → two receivers.
- **L6 Two Voices** — splitter + reflector + absorber + forbidden receiver.
- **L7 Late Echo** — introduce Delay Plate; receiver B one beat after A. Timing
  visualization must be explicit.
- **L8 Harmonic Gate** — introduce Resonator; two pulses in a defined relationship.
  Logic-based, no music theory.

## Series C — Phase
- **L9 Crossing Echoes** — two emitters; crossing paths do not interfere (rule
  shown explicitly).
- **L10 Double Beat** — splitter + delay + resonator; simple timing pattern.
- **L11 Resonance Network** — reflector + splitter + absorber + delay + multiple
  receivers. Readability + performance milestone.
- **L12 Final Resonance** — deliberate culmination; elegant, not a checklist dump.
  Completion → CHAMBER IN TUNE.
