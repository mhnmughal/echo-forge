# Privacy Policy for Resonance Chamber

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Resonance Chamber is a single player puzzle game published by Rehman Mughal
(internal codename "Echo Forge"). This policy explains what the app does and does
not do with information.

## Summary

Resonance Chamber does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It works fully
offline.

## Information the app collects

None. Resonance Chamber does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location,
photos, or any other personal information. It does not create an account and
there is no login.

## Device permissions

Resonance Chamber requests no device permissions. It does not use the camera, the
microphone, location services, Bluetooth, the photo library, or advertising and
tracking identifiers. The game is about acoustics, but it does not listen to the
microphone.

## Networking

Resonance Chamber makes no network connections during normal use. There is no analytics,
no advertising, no crash reporting service, no cloud save, and no third party
software development kit. Unity engine diagnostics and analytics are disabled in
the project.

## Local data

Your progress, which chambers you have completed, your ratings, and your audio and
haptic settings are saved in a small file on your device. The app does not
transmit this file. Your operating system may include app data in device backups,
according to your device settings. Deleting the app removes its local copy.

## Children

Resonance Chamber contains no ads, no purchases, no chat, and no links out of the app.
The final age rating is assigned through App Store Connect; it is not confirmed yet.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same location
with a new date at the top.
