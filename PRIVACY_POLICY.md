# Privacy Policy for Resonance Chamber

Last updated: {{LAST_UPDATED_DATE}}

Resonance Chamber is a single player puzzle game published by Resonance Chamber Co
(internal codename "Echo Forge"). This policy explains what the app does and does
not do with information.

## Summary

Resonance Chamber does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It works
fully offline.

## Information the app collects

None. Resonance Chamber does not ask for your name, email address, contacts,
location, photos, or any other personal information. It does not create an account
and there is no login. The app is not directed at children, and it collects no
data from anyone regardless of age.

## Device permissions

Resonance Chamber requests no device permissions. It does not use the camera, the
microphone, location services, Bluetooth, the photo library, or advertising and
tracking identifiers. The game is about acoustics, but it does not listen to the
microphone.

## Networking

Resonance Chamber makes no network connections during normal use. There is no
analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting service, no cloud save, and no third
party software development kit. Unity engine diagnostics and analytics are
disabled in the project.

## Local data

Your progress, which chambers you have completed, your ratings, and your audio and
haptic settings are saved in a small file on your device. The app does not
transmit this file. Your operating system may include app data in device backups,
according to your device settings. Deleting the app removes its local copy.

## Tracking

Resonance Chamber does not track you and does not use the App Tracking Transparency
framework, because it has nothing to track. No data is shared with third parties,
because there are no third parties.

## Children

Resonance Chamber contains no ads, no purchases, no chat, and no links out of the
app during play. The age rating is assigned through App Store Connect.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted at the same public address
with a new date at the top.
