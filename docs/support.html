Resonance Chamber is a calm, fully offline puzzle game. You shape a room, release one pulse of sound, and route it to every listener. This page covers how to play and the questions that come up most often.

How to play

  1. Tap START SESSION on the main menu to begin the Sound Check, a short guided tutorial.
  2. Tap a bronze component to select it. Tap a selected reflector again to rotate it. Tap a highlighted socket to move a movable part.
  3. Tap the round PULSE button to run the room and watch the wavefront travel.
  4. Reach every receiver, and satisfy any timing or resonance rule, to resolve the chamber and unlock the next one.
  5. RESONANCE DIAL jumps between unlocked chambers. SOUND GUIDE explains every component. TUNING holds volume, haptics, and a full progress reset.

Common questions

The game has no sound

Check the two faders in TUNING on the main menu, and make sure your device is not in silent mode. Resonance Chamber respects the iOS silent switch.

My progress disappeared

Progress is saved on your device only. It is lost if the app is deleted, if the device is reset, or if you tap RESET RESONANCE HISTORY in TUNING. There is no account and no cloud save, so progress does not move between devices.

A chamber seems impossible

Every chamber has a solution and nothing is random. If a pulse misses, the result screen tells you why (a receiver was missed, a forbidden receiver was hit, the timing was off, or a resonance was incomplete). Open the SOUND GUIDE for a refresher on how each part behaves, then keep tuning. There is no game over.

The screen only works in landscape

That is intended. Each puzzle is laid out wide, so the game is landscape only.

Does the game work offline

Yes, completely. Resonance Chamber never connects to the internet. It has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. See the privacy policy.

Haptics are too strong or not working

Toggle haptics in TUNING. On devices without a haptic motor there is no effect.
